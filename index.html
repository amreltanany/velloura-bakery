A few things you knead to know.

Get in touch
Do I need to pre-order?

Not for everyday bakes, they’re on the counter while they last. For catering and larger orders, ask at least 48 hours ahead.

Can I collect a pastry box?

Yes. Tell us what you’d like and when, and we’ll have a box packed and waiting at the counter.

Will my favourite be available?

We bake fresh each morning and sell out most days. Come early for the best selection.

What about dietary requirements?

Ask at the counter. We can tell you what’s in every bake and what is made without dairy, eggs or nuts.
